Hi, I'm Rohan. I build things that have to work in the real world.

I started out building robots. Today I build agentic AI for healthcare, where software has to be just as reliable as a robot on a competition field.

It began in high school with FRC Team 1746. As captain I wrote PID loops, wrangled our code, and helped the team reach 20th in the world. That turned into a job keeping warehouse robots running at Automation Direct, and later into leading Micromouse at RoboJackets, where I still design PCBs and maze-solving firmware.

Robots made me curious about how machines see and decide, so I moved into research. I trained vision models to catch manufacturing defects, built a chatbot that runs CFD simulations on its own, and found a two-gene signal for early osteoarthritis.

Then I went to work on healthcare payments at Waystar and saw how much of healthcare runs on slow handoffs between people and systems. That's what I work on now: AI agents for those handoffs, built carefully enough that clinicians can trust them.
